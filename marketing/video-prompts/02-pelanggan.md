# Versi 2 — Untuk pelanggan cuci (B2C)

**Durasi:** 20 detik · **Rasio:** 9:16 (utama, untuk Reels/TikTok) dan 16:9 · **Tujuan:** pelanggan booking cuci online.

Setiap prompt di bawah: tambahkan blok dari [style-global.md](style-global.md) di akhirnya. Untuk 9:16, tambahkan juga blok vertikal.

---

## Shot 1 — Hook: mobil kotor & malas antre (0–3 dtk)

```
A cute stylized 3D car covered in mud splashes and dust, parked on an
off-white ground, with a small grey rain cloud above it. Next to it, a long
line of identical tiny cars stretches into the distance. The car slumps its
suspension as if sighing. Camera slowly pushes in. Playful, slightly comic.
```

- 🎙️ VO: *"Mobil kotor, tapi malas antre lama?"*
- 🖊️ Teks: **Malas antre cuci mobil?**

## Shot 2 — Booking dari HP (3–8 dtk)

```
A smartphone floats into frame with a smooth spring animation. On its screen,
rounded cards show car-type icons; one gets tapped and glows vanilla. Then a
row of time-slot pills appears; one is tapped and turns Deep Mocha. A tiny
calendar icon with a checkmark pops up with a bounce. Soap bubbles drift.
Smooth dolly-in on the phone.
```

- 🎙️ VO: *"Pilih jenis kendaraan, paket, dan jamnya — langsung dari HP."*
- 🖊️ Teks: **Booking online dalam 1 menit**

## Shot 3 — Konfirmasi WhatsApp (8–11 dtk)

```
A green chat bubble with a double checkmark slides out of the phone and
grows, glowing softly. Small confetti made of water droplets bursts around
it. Camera gently orbits the floating bubble.
```

- 🎙️ VO: *"Konfirmasinya masuk lewat WhatsApp."*
- 🖊️ Teks: **Konfirmasi via WhatsApp**

## Shot 4 — Cuci tanpa antre & pantau langsung (11–16 dtk)

```
The muddy car drives smoothly into a bright, minimal wash bay; thick white
snow-foam covers it in a satisfying wave, then a gentle water rinse washes it
off, revealing a glossy, sparkling car. Picture-in-picture: a phone shows a
progress bar filling from left to right in Pale Sky color in sync with the
wash. Smooth tracking shot alongside the car.
```

- 🎙️ VO: *"Datang sesuai jam, langsung dicuci — dan pantau prosesnya dari HP."*
- 🖊️ Teks: **Tanpa antre · Pantau langsung**

## Shot 5 — CTA (16–20 dtk)

```
The clean car rolls forward and stops center frame, sparkling with a soft
glint. The grey cloud from the start turns into a small sun. Behind the car,
a Deep Mocha rounded card scales in with a soft bounce; a Vanilla Custard
pill-shaped button pulses gently with a glow. Bubbles float upward. Camera
completely still for the final 2 seconds for text overlay.
```

- 🎙️ VO: *"Kinclong tanpa ribet. Booking sekarang di Sparkle Wash!"*
- 🖊️ Teks end card:
  - Judul: **Kinclong tanpa antre.**
  - Tombol: **Booking Sekarang →**
  - Kecil: *Link di bio · sparklewash.id/booking*

---

## Audio

- **Musik:** pop ceria dan ringan, 110–120 BPM, drop kecil saat busa menutup mobil di Shot 4.
- **SFX:** "tap" lembut saat tombol ditekan, notifikasi WhatsApp di Shot 3, "splash/foam" di Shot 4, "sparkle" + "ding" di akhir.
- **Suara VO:** ramah, santai, sedikit lebih cepat dari versi pemilik usaha.

## Variasi CTA (untuk A/B test)

- **"Booking Sekarang →"** — langsung ke halaman booking.
- **"Cuci pertama diskon 20%"** — jika ada kode promo (fitur promo Sparkle Wash).
- **"Kumpulkan stempel, cuci ke-10 gratis"** — untuk menonjolkan program loyalitas.
