# Versi 1 — Untuk pemilik usaha cuci (B2B)

**Durasi:** 30 detik · **Rasio:** 16:9 (versi 9:16 di bawah) · **Tujuan:** pemilik usaha mau mencoba aplikasi.

Setiap prompt di bawah: tambahkan blok dari [style-global.md](style-global.md) di akhirnya.

---

## Shot 1 — Hook: masalah (0–5 dtk)

```
A chaotic car wash counter rendered as a miniature 3D diorama: messy paper
notebook, scattered receipts, a long queue of tiny stylized cars waiting,
small red clock icons ticking fast. Camera slowly pushes in from a high angle.
Papers flutter. Mood slightly stressful but playful. Muted mocha tones,
desaturated.
```

- 🎙️ VO: *"Antrean panjang, catatan berantakan, uang masuk nggak jelas?"*
- 🖊️ Teks: **Masih pakai buku catatan?**

## Shot 2 — Transisi: solusi muncul (5–10 dtk)

```
A giant soap bubble floats into frame and pops; the burst of foam wipes the
messy scene clean, transforming it into a bright off-white space. From the
foam, a sleek laptop and a smartphone rise and rotate smoothly into place,
screens glowing vanilla and pale sky. Seamless match-cut transition, smooth
continuous camera orbit.
```

- 🎙️ VO: *"Kenalin, Sparkle Wash — sistem kasir cuci kendaraan yang bikin semuanya rapi."*
- 🖊️ Teks: **Sparkle Wash**

## Shot 3 — Fitur: antrean & kasir (10–15 dtk)

```
Close-up of a floating 3D dashboard UI: three columns of rounded cards
(waiting, washing, done). Small stylized car cards slide smoothly from one
column to the next with satisfying snap animations. A vanilla-colored "pay"
button gets pressed and a coin with a checkmark pops out. Camera glides
laterally.
```

- 🎙️ VO: *"Catat kendaraan cukup dari nomor polisi, pantau antrean, dan terima pembayaran tunai sampai QRIS."*
- 🖊️ Teks: **Antrean real-time · Tunai & QRIS**

## Shot 4 — Fitur: booking online & WhatsApp (15–20 dtk)

```
A smartphone floats center frame showing a calendar grid of time-slot pills;
one pill gets tapped and glows mocha. A green chat bubble with a checkmark
flies out of the phone and lands on a second phone, which shows a progress
bar filling up smoothly from 0 to 100%. Soap bubbles drift around. Smooth
dolly-in.
```

- 🎙️ VO: *"Pelanggan bisa booking online, dapat notifikasi WhatsApp, dan pantau cuciannya langsung dari HP."*
- 🖊️ Teks: **Booking online · Notifikasi WhatsApp · Lacak cucian**

## Shot 5 — Fitur: tim & laporan (20–25 dtk)

```
A tablet shows a checklist with large rounded checkboxes ticking one by one
with a bouncy animation. The camera pulls back and the tablet transforms into
a floating report: bar chart columns grow smoothly upward in pale sky color,
a large number counter rolls up. Confetti-like water droplets sparkle.
```

- 🎙️ VO: *"Checklist untuk pencuci, komisi terhitung otomatis, dan laporan pendapatan siap dibaca kapan saja."*
- 🖊️ Teks: **Checklist · Komisi otomatis · Laporan bulanan**

## Shot 6 — CTA (25–30 dtk)

```
Clean off-white background. A shiny stylized car drives in smoothly, stops
center frame and sparkles as if freshly washed. Behind it, a large Deep Mocha
rounded card scales in with a soft bounce; a Vanilla Custard pill-shaped
button pulses gently with a soft glow, inviting a tap. Bubbles float upward.
Camera completely still for the final 2 seconds for text overlay.
```

- 🎙️ VO: *"Saatnya usaha cuci Anda naik level. Coba Sparkle Wash sekarang!"*
- 🖊️ Teks end card:
  - Judul: **Usaha cuci lebih rapi, pelanggan lebih puas.**
  - Tombol: **Coba Gratis 14 Hari →** *(alternatif: "Chat via WhatsApp")*
  - Kecil: *wa.me/62xxxxxxxxxx · sparklewash.id*

## Audio

- **Musik:** upbeat lo-fi pop / corporate ringan, 100–110 BPM. Shot 1 agak tegang, berubah "lega" saat gelembung pecah di Shot 2.
- **SFX:** "pop" gelembung di tiap transisi, "whoosh" halus saat kartu berpindah, "ding" saat bayar / checklist dicentang, "sparkle" di mobil akhir.
- **Suara VO:** hangat, santai tapi meyakinkan, tempo sedang.
